============================================================
OBSERVATION
============================================================

The first fine-tuning experiment did not improve the MFT
Transformer's performance.

The changes increased dropout from 0.15 to 0.25 and changed
the AdamW learning rate from 0.0002 to 0.0001 and weight decay
from 0.0001 to 0.0003.

The best validation accuracy was 49.81%, while the final test
accuracy was 47.52%.

Therefore, this configuration was not retained as the final
MFT Transformer configuration.

The baseline configuration remains the reference experiment.
